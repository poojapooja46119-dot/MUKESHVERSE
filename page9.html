Dear Mukesh ❤️

Firstuh firstuh una paathappo, nee eppovum ippadi dhaan happy-ah iruppiya nu nenachen... 🥰 Neetha first time en kitta vandhu pesuna... School padikkum pothu bus-la irukkumbothu neetha en kitta vandhu pesuna. Naan appolam yaaru kittayum pesa maaten. Yenna naan andha school-ku pudhusu, oru maari nervous-ah iruppen. Appo bus-la irukkumbothu neetha en kitta first time pesuna. First time pesumbodhe romba naal friend kitta pesura maari pesuna... 🫶 Aana idhu unakku nyabagam irukkuma nu theriyala, enakku sariya nyabagam irukku... Appo enakku konjam happy-ah irundhuchu. Paravala, inga ellarum nalla dhaan pesuranga nu nenachen... ❤️ Athukku apram appadiye insta-la pesunom nu nenakkuren, sariya theriyala... But nee evlo kashtapaduva nu naan nenachikooda paakala. Apram enna nambi unnodaya ella feelings-um share panna... 🥺 Idhu maari yaarachu en kitta pesuna enakku enna panradhu nu sariya theriyadhu... Aana edho, "Nee edhuvum feel pannadha, pathukalam, vidu" nu mattum dhaan solla mudiyum... 🫂 Unna edhachum hurt pannirundha illa kashtapaduthura maari naan edhachum pannirundha sorry... Apdi edhachum naan pannirundha nee en kitta sollu. Naan adha change pannikkuren, okay-va? ❤️ Yaaru enna solluvaanga, adha solluvaanga nu nenaikadha. Unakku enna thonutho adha pannu, sariya? Eppovum yaaru pesuradhayum kekkatha. Even naane sonnalum seri, unakku enna thonutho adha pannu... ✨ Ellame oru naal change aagum... Adhe maari heal aagurathukkum time edukkum. Enna, nee paathirundha kooda en Insta bio-la naan potiruppen, "Don't feel, only heal" nu... 🌸 Adhu maari sari aaga time edukkum... Pathukalam, edhu vandhaalum namma paathukalam. ❤️🫶
